# AIRI: removed from the public site

On 2026-10-31 JST, the site owner requested that AIRI be excluded from Market Note.

- The public `/airi/` page and desktop, footer, and mobile navigation links are removed.
- The external Saturday 09:00 Asia/Tokyo AIRI update automation was disabled on the same date. No replacement schedule was created.
- `input.json`, `releases/`, `app/airi-data.json`, the model and collector scripts, and existing research history are retained unchanged. Retention in this repository does not mean the feature remains published on GitHub Pages.
- `AIRI_UPDATE.md` describes the former update procedure; it is not an instruction to resume publication. Do not recreate the route, navigation, or schedule without a new explicit request from the owner.
- Other Market Note features and editorial schedules remain unchanged.
